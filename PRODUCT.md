# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, often on a phone, often for under a minute:

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** checking technical fit and whether the claims are real. Softjit is open to full-stack or mobile roles.
- **Local business owners** in Amritsar and across India (shops, restaurants, salons, clinics, wholesalers) who want a website or app and need a clear price and a fast way to get in touch (WhatsApp first).

## Product purpose

Turn a short visit into an interview request or a client enquiry by showing all 20 shipped projects quickly, proving them with real screenshots, numbers and code, and making contact one tap away.

## Brand personality

Confident, curious, a little magical. Like meeting an engineer who keeps a star atlas on his desk: precise about the work, warm about the people it's for. Never arrogant, never salesy.

## Concept

"The Astral Observatory": every project is a star on Softjit's chart. The theme lives in the visuals (constellation, astrolabe, gold ornaments, stardust) — never in the copy.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-template portfolios: glow blobs, glassmorphism cards, gradient-text names, logo marquees.
- Costume themes: parchment textures, gothic fonts, "spells" and "quests" in the copy.
- Unverifiable claims, fake testimonials, inflated metrics, a former job shown as current.

## Design principles

- **Breadth first, depth on demand** — every project visible within a few scrolls; case studies, code and write-ups one tap deeper.
- **Show, don't claim** — whole screenshots in real device frames, numbers with receipts, real code.
- **Magic in the details** — novelty and motion to hold attention (constellation, reveals, cursor glow), but copy stays plain.
- **Two audiences, one site** — technical proof on the homepage, plain pricing on /hire, WhatsApp everywhere a business owner looks.
- **Honest by default** — if a fact can't be verified from the projects, it doesn't go on the site.

## Accessibility and inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast for text (cream/mist on night). Gold is decorative only.
- Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.
- Colour is never the only signal (mint/amber are paired with "web application"/"mobile app" labels).
- 44px minimum tap targets; no horizontal scrolling at 375px; constellation labels stay on screen and are tappable.
